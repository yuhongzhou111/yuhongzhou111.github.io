Biography

From antibody science to medicines.

Dr. Yuhong Zhou (周宇虹) is a biopharmaceutical scientist and entrepreneur, co-founder of BlissBio, and the company’s Chief Scientific Officer. Public company materials describe her responsibilities as leading preclinical research and development management and technological innovation.

Her career has centered on translating immunology and antibody research into therapeutic approaches. At Morphotek, she was Senior Director for Antibody Development from August 2004 to April 2016. Earlier in her career, she completed postdoctoral research at the Roche Institute with Thomas Curran. Her peer-reviewed research includes human monoclonal antibody discovery, tumor-associated endosialin (CD248), anti-GD2 antibodies, antibody-mediated antitumor activity, and antibody-drug conjugate engineering.

At BlissBio, Zhou joined at the company’s founding in December 2017. Patent records identify her as a co-inventor on antibody and ADC inventions assigned to BlissBio, including tetravalent ADCs and engineered antibodies directed at cancer targets.

This biography is based on public company and academic records. Invention listings identify co-inventors and assignees; they do not imply sole authorship or personal ownership of company-assigned patents.

02 · Tumor microenvironment

Endosialin / TEM1 / CD248

Research explored endosialin interactions with extracellular-matrix proteins and antibody-mediated internalization as a potential approach to disrupting tumor microvasculature.

GD2 and folate receptor alpha
