About

Building science into medicines.

Dr. Yuhong Zhou (周宇虹) is a biopharmaceutical scientist and entrepreneur, and the co-founder and Chief Science Officer of BlissBio. Dr. Zhou has more than 25 years of research and development experience in targeted therapy and immunotherapy.

Before co-founding BlissBio, Dr. Zhou served as senior director for antibody development at Morphotek from August 2004 to April 2016. At BlissBio, she is primarily responsible for preclinical R&D management and technological innovation.

Dr. Zhou received a bachelor’s degree in biochemistry and a master’s degree in genetics from Fudan University, followed by a Ph.D. in microbiology and molecular genetics from Rutgers University.
